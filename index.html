Four Plate Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Four Plate is an iPhone app for logging your lifts and seeing how your crew is doing. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't track you, we don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a user ID. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email and ID they share with us. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the relay address.
  • Your profile: your display name, catchphrase and plate colour.
  • Your lifts: the personal records you log (lift, weight and date).
  • Safety records: reports you file about other lifters (the reason and any details you write) and the list of lifters you've blocked.
  • On your phone only: a few app settings and your sign-in session, kept in the app's local storage on your device.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no analytics or advertising code.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your profile and lifts to other signed-in Four Plate users. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • To review reports and keep the app free of abuse.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts the app's database and accounts. Everything above, apart from what stays on your phone, is stored there.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to the accounts you hold with them.
  • Slack receives an alert when someone files a report, so we can review it quickly. The alert contains the display names and user IDs of the reporter and the reported lifter, the reason, and any details written in the report. It never contains email addresses.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete it, open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your account, profile, lifts and blocks. If you signed in with Apple, it also disconnects Four Plate from your Apple ID.

Reports you filed are kept so we can finish reviewing them, with your name and ID removed. Reports filed about you are deleted along with your account. Report alerts already sent to our private moderation channel in Slack may stay there until we remove them.

You can also ask us by email to see, correct or delete your data, and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Four Plate isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
